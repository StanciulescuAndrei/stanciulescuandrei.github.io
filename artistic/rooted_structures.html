About This Project

An immersive performance project engaging with accumulation, construction, and constant growth, framed within a dystopian reflection on consumerism and expansion. The work invites reflection on care, interconnectedness, and symbiosis, without imposing a fixed narrative.

The project offers an immersive environment that encourages the audience to engage attentively and form their own interpretations. Moment, body, and technology create this immersive space where we address the questions of growth, care, rush, attention, and future. It is a digital interactive performance combining technology and dance performance using improvisation. Improvisation because there is no answer to our questions yet. We explore, try and try again as we do in our lives.

Developed in collaboration with Zsanna Sike, Mangos Fanni, Critian Dubineanschi, Barbora Molcikova, Maria-Karina Farkas, Tiago Machado, Andres Laguia, and Aron Telegdy.

Artistic Concept

We want to address an issue of infinite growth, the idea of having more and more “do not rest just work”. The system that came up from a capitalistic dream. The structure that does not care, that makes us do not care about others, that wants us to focus only on getting better, having more. The system where we miss care. And care and caring for others is our second premises. We are missing care, but what happens when you have too much care?

What happens when you care about others but not about yourself. It is not like care is the key. Because care without limits can destroy us and others as well. If we focus on caring for others and it becomes our purpose in life, we put on them so much care that we lock them in cages made from caring. They will lose their freedom and we will burn out.

So, what is the answer to this? We don't have it. We invite you to think about these questions with us tonight and maybe even after the show. Because we believe that we need to think about it and try to find some solutions because we are living in a limited world, and we cannot live in the dream of an unlimited system anymore. But what all of us can do so far for now is to observe, notice, and ask people around us, be patient, be gentle and respect the limits. of the data, in this case, the image and the moving rectangle, and the sound, instead of the sound being preprogrammed.
