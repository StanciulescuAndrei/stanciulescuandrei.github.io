Master's Thesis

Accelerating 3D Gaussian Splat Rendering

Andrei Stanciulescu

Universitat Politècnica de Catalunya • October 2024

The 3D Gaussian Splatting method for 3D environment reconstruction from images brought significant advancements to photorealistic novel-view synthesis. It combines the advantages of primitive-based rendering with a differentiable renderer, thus obtaining state-of-the-art image quality and surpassing neural methods for scene representation in optimization and rendering speed. This project presents a method for accelerating 3DGS rendering through a hierarchical Level of Detail structure that combines a regular octree subdivision with feature-based primitive clustering.

Bachelor's Thesis

Decision Support System for Liver Lesion Segmentation Based on Advanced Convolutional Neural Network Architectures

Popescu Dan, Andrei Stanciulescu, Pomohaci Mihai Dan, Ichim Loretta

MDPI Bioengineering • September 2022

An intelligent decision system for segmenting liver and hepatic tumors by integrating four efficient neural networks (ResNet152, ResNeXt101, DenseNet201, and InceptionV3). Images from computed tomography for training, validation, and testing were taken from the public LiTS17 database and preprocessed to better highlight liver tissue and tumors.

Multi-Energy and Fast-Convergence Iterative Reconstruction Algorithm for Organic Material Identification Using X-ray Computed Tomography

Iovea Mihai, Andrei Stanciulescu, Hermann Edward, Neagu Marian, Duliu Octavian G.

MDPI Materials • February 2023

A specialized multi-stage procedure based on a multi-energy computed tomography investigation within the 20–160 keV domain. The procedure consists of compensation for beam hardening and other non-linear effects, a 3D fast reconstruction algorithm, and algorithms for simultaneous determination of the density and effective atomic number. This enabled determining both values in approximately 24 seconds with accuracy and precision of less than 3%.
